Memorandum
when the Negroes
of Joseph Dorsey
were recorded

Recorded 18th October 1780

[back]

By the Records of Westmoreland County it appears that the Negroes
of Joseph Dorsey as follows, viz

Charles, Phill, Aaron, Tom, Casse, Jane, Pompey, Rachel
Phillis, George, Frederick, James & Harmer were recorded
1780 October 18th
