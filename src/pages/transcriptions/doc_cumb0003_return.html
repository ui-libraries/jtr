No 3
22d Augt 1780

[back]

Ralph Starret of Rye Township in Cumberland
County & State of Pennsylvania Farmer hath this day
delivered in writing to the clk of the Peace for sd County
the Names of all his Negroe and Mullatoe Slaves viz:
Jacob a Negroe man aged about 21 years
Grace a Negroe Woman aged about 15 years
Augt 22d 1780 Ralph Starret
