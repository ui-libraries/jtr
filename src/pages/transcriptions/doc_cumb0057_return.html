Hopewell Township Cumberland County
The 10th Octr 1780
Robert Culbertson Farmer
in the Township & County afosaid
make return of his name occupation
and place of Residence to the Clerk
of the peace of said County ~also~
a return of the Negroes Slaves owned
by him together With their ages & Sexes

Viz: Jerrit a Negroe Man aged 21 years
Nell a Negroe Woman aged 20
Ben a Boy aged 8 years
Jake a Boy aged one year

Robert Culbertson
