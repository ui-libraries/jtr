Editor’s Note. The section commencing “thomas akin” is written perpendicular to the return heading along the left-hand edge. It appears that James McCune tore and repurposed an older legal document to compose his registration of Chloe.

James McCune & Ala one of the Execrs of
Alexr Bryans decd returns the following
Negro Slave as the property of sd decd Estate
to be recorded agreeable to Law viz
Chloe a Female Negro aged about 50 years
to be free in 9 yrs from the 10th May 1777

30th Octr 1780

James McCune Exr
