40
6th October 1780
Col. Fredk Watts

[back]

To William Lyon Esquire Clerk of the peace for
the County of Cumberland

I the Subscriber of Rye Town Ship Cumberland
County do in conformity to an Act of General Assembly of
Pennsylvania passed the first day of March 1780 for the Gradual
Abolition of Slavery return in order to be recorded, a Mulatto
Servant Wench named Jemima aged Eighteen years the Sixteenth
day of February Last Witness my hand this 4th day of October 1780
October 1780

Frederick Watts
