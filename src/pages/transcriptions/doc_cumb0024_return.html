28
21st Sepr 1780
John Creigh’s return
of 1 Slave

[back]

In Pursuance of the Act for gradual Absolution
of Slavery John Creigh of the Town of Carlisle
in the County of Cumberland Storekeeper
doth hereby enter with the Clerk of the Sessions
of the said county, Jean a Negroe Woman
aged about Twenty or whom said John
owns As a Slave during her Natural
Life
