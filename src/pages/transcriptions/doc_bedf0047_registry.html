Bedford 1st January 1828

Humphrey Dillon of the borough of Bedford, and State
of Pennsylvania, possessed a male black child, born the 23d day
of August last, named Charles, son of Eliza Diggs, a female
Negro servant of the said Humphrey Dillon, until the age of
twenty eight years Enters the same with the Clerk of the Court
of Quarter Sessions of the peace of the said County, agreeably
to the act of assembly passed the 29th day of March 1788 which said
child Charles the said Humphrey Dillon claims as his servant, until he
arrives at the age of twenty eight years.

Bedford County ss. The above named Humphrey Dillon being duly
sworn deposeth and saith that the facts stated in the above return
or account, are true to the best of his knowledge

Sworn & Subscribed the 18th Jany 1828
Before Job Mann Register

H Dillon
