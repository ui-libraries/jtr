Abraham Locbridge of the Town of Carlisle
Store-Keeper Returns

Hannah a female Negroe Slave Aged 35 Ys

To John Agnew Esqr
30 th Octr 1780

Abm Locbridge

269, 270
Abm Loughbridge &
Wm Culberson

Editor’s Note. The heading, which commences “269, 270,” is scrawled upside down at the bottom of the return. The scan only includes Abraham’s registration of Hannah.
