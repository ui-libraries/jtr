[front]

To John Agnew Esqr

78
12th Octr 1780
Patrick Maxwell
Esqr

[back]

Sir/
I made a Return sometime ago of My
Neagroes to Mr Lyon, but I am enformed since,
that you are the Recorder, I therfor send you
this one
1 Neagroe fellow 31 years old named Cato
1 Neagro Wench 35 years named Jean
1 Do Girl 12 Do Cloe
1 Do Boy 2 Do Nicolas

from your Humble Servt
Octr the 7th 1780

Pattr Maxwell
