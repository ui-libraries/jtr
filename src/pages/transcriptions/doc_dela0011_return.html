[front]

Mary Calhoun’s
Return of a Negro
Child

[back]

I Mary Calhoun of the township of Aston
in Delaware County Comonwealth of Pennsyla
do Mak Return to the Clerk of the Peace of the Said
County upon oath a ^ female Negro Child named
Margaret Reddon born the fifth day of April
last in order that the said Child maybe
entered & Registered according to the
provisions of the act ^ Assembly in Such Case made
& provided

Sworn & Subscribed
Septr 22nd 1809 before me
Jos Engle Clk.

Mary Calhoon
