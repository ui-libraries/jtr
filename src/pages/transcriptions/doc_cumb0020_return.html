24
14th Sepr 1780
Geeo Brown
List of Negroes &c

[back]

A list of Negroe & Mullata Slaves, with their names &
ages, the property of the subscriber of Cumbd County, in as follows
Viz

Negro Jack aged about thirty five years
Negroe Dick aged about twenty five years
Mulatta Sampson aged about seventeen years
Mulatta Silvey aged about thirty five years
Negroe Judy aged about twenty five years
Negroe Phillis aged about thirteen years
Mulatta Minto aged seven years
Mulatta Peter aged five years
Negroe Phillip aged years
Negroe Doll aged two years

Geo Brown
Augt 21st 1780
