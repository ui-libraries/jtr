29
22 Sepr 1780
Robt Miller
list of Slaves

[back]

In pursuance of the 'Act for the gradual
Abolition of Slavery" Robert Miller of the Town
of Carlisle in the County of Cumberland Tanner
doth hereby enter with the Clerk of the Sessions
of the said County

Darby ^ aged 40 yrs & Violet his wife ^ Slaves aged about 40
1 Toby a slave aged 40 yrs
Abraham a slave 4 years

To
John Agnew Esqr
