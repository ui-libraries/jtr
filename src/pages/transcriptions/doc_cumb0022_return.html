26
21st Sepr 1780
John Davidson
West Pennso
return of 1 Negro

[back]

In Pursuance of the Act for the gradual
Abolition of Slavery: John Davison of the
Township of Westpennsborough in the County
of Cumberland Farmer doth hereby Enter with
the Clark of the Sessions of the said County

Bill a Negro Man aged 25 yrs
years or there Abouts whom he owns as
A Slave During his Life

To John Agnew Esqur
Clerk of the Peace in
for the County of
Cumberland

Septr 21st 1780
