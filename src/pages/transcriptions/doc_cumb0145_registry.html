24th Octr 1780

159 Capt James McFarland West Penns Farmr
456 Jack a Male Negro Slave for Life X 28 yrs
457 Venis a Female Negro Slave do X 25 do
458 Doll a Female Negro Slave do X 14 do
459 Ned a Male Negro Slave do X 12 do
460 Cezar a Male Negro Slave do X 9 do
461 Pompey a Male Negro Slave do X 6 do
462 Dick a Male Negro Slave do X 3 do
463 Dinah aFe-Male Negro Slave do X 8 months

Editor’s Note. For Dinah’s registration, “Fe” is written over the letter “a” so that it is appended to “Male.”
