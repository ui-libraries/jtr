William Thompson
of the Township of New London Chester County
Miller returns to the Clerk of the Peace for the
said County the Name and Age of his Mulatto
Boy, to wit a Mulatto Boy named Lawson,
born the fourth day of July Anno Domini 1794
William Thompson on his solemn Oath
according to Law doth depose and say that the
above return so by him Made is Just and true
according to the best f his Knowledge & beleif

Sworn & Subscribed
Aug. 22d 1794 Before
William Gibbons Clk.

William Thompson
