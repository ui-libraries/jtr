List of Slaves

[back]

A list of Negro & Mullatto slaves & Servants with their
ages Names & ages, the property of the subscriber of Antrim
Township, Cumbd County, in as follows Viz
Negro Mocky aged about thirty five years
Mulatto Nan aged about 30 years
— Petter age 20
— Pady age 15
Jacob age 5 y & 9 mths
Joe age 2 10 mths > 31 years
Fabbey age 4 mths
Haddy age 7 6 mts

Agt 23d 1780
James Poe
