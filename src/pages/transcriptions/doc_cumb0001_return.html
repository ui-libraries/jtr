N1
22d Augt 1780
Francis West Esqr.
Return of Slaves

[back]

Francis West of Tyrone Township in Cumberland
County & State of Pennsylvania Esquire hath
this Day delivered in Writing to Clerk of the Peace
for the said County the Names of all his
Mullatoe & Negro Slaves Viz-
Sligoe a Negroe aged about 50 Years
Jacob a mullato for Life abt. 34 Do
Poll a Negro Woman abt. 23 Do
Chamont a Mullatoe abt. 9 Do for Life
Milla a Mullatoe Girle abt 3 Do for Life
Lewis a Mestoe male child born November 10th. 1779 }
John & Deb A Negro abt 29 Years for Life
Debby about 50 Years a Negro Woman
August 22d
Frans West
Entered
