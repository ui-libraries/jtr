3d Octr 1780
John Harris Esqr
Fermanagh

[back]

Sr according to the Law for the
gradual abolition ^ of Slavery all Negro
and mulato Slaves are to be Registrated
Registered Before the first of Novr
next I have got two they are
Both famels the oldest about 14
years of ages her name is Meela
the other about II her name is
Amee you will please Register
^ them names as the Law Directs
I am Sr your most obedt sert

John Harris

Octr 2d 1780

Editor’s Note. The address “Mr Wm Lyon, Carlisle” is included perpendicular to the left the main text.
