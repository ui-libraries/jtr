William Thompson of Hamilton Township in the County
of Cumberland Miller & Farmer Returns the following Negro’s
to be registred agreeable to Law vizt

Sall a Nergro Slave for Life aged about 22 yrs of age
Patt a Mullatto Slave for Life 5 do

13th Octr 1780

To John Agnew clk of Sessions
in Cumbd County
William Thompson
