Hopewell Township Cumberland County
The 10th October 1780

Robert Henry practitioner in Physic in
the Township and County aforesaid make a
Return of name occupation and place of
residence to the clerk of the peace of said
county - also a retun of the negroe slaves
owned by him together with their ages and
sexes

Viz Cynthia a negroe girl aged 11 years

Robert Henry

Hopewell Township Cumberland County
The 10th October 1780

Kezia Bainbridge in the Township
and County aforesaid makes return of her
name and pace of residence to the clerk of the
peace of said county - also a return of the
negroe slaves owned by her together with
their ages and sexes

Viz Flora a negroe Girl aged 10 years
Kezia Bainbridge
