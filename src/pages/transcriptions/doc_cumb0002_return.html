22d Augt. 1780
Robert Gibson
Return of Slaves

[back]

Robert Gibson of Carlisle in the County of Cumberland
pencilvania Came before one John Agnew Justice of the
peace for said County and Delivered to me the names and
age of all his sleaves and desires the same be recorded
as such
phillis a Mullato sleave for Life blind     of both Eyes
aged about 25 or 26 years of age
poll a Mustee sleave for life aged 3 years old and
one month
22 August 1780           Robert Gibson Carpenter
