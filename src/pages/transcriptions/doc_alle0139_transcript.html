No. 166.

Abner Barker of the Borough of Pittsburgh
on his solmen oath doth depose & say that
about the 16th Day of Aug 1809, Charletto
a Mullatto servent of Mrs Maria Butler,
was Delivered of a Mullattoe Male Child
named William and desires that the said
child may be registered Agreeably to Law
Abner Barker Sworn & subcribed the
12 of December 1809… E. Pentland Pro.
