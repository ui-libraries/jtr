Cumberland County Letterkenny Tp a
return of my Negroes with their names
Ages and sexes as follows Viz

Sampson a Negroe Slave aged 30 yrs
Will a Nege Slave aged 24 do
Cuff a do do aged 16 do
Doll a Mullatoe during life 15 do
Beck a Mullatoe during life 5 do

Carlisle 22 Sepr 1780
Jno Agnew Esqr
James Finley
