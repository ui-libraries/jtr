No. 60.

Tom More Born 30th July 1793 belonging
to your humble Servent. Andrew McFarlane

James Brisson Clk. Q.S…
A County January 27th 1794

A McFarlane of Mifflen Township Sworn to
the truth of the entry before me

James Brisson

continuation of the above

Sir
I have made several attempts to visit your
office at Pittsburgh in order to Record
a Mullatto Male Child Named Tom More Born
30th July 1793. it being the Sone of aslave
for life by law    The time being Nearly Expired
has sent my wife who was Present when he was
Born in order if Proof is necessary she can
be qualefied to the time. I am Sir with James
Brisson Clk Q.S. Respect yours Se
Jany 27th 1794 A. McFarlane
