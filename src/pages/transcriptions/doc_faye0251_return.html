Registry of Alfred
a Mulato child
by Israel Miller

Filed 21st Aug. 1816

[back]

Israel Miller of Redstone Township ^, Fayette County, Pennsylvania Merchant
Returns for registry a mulato Male Child named
Alfred, born 2d day of April 1816, returned
21st. August 1816.

Israel Miller

Sworn Affirmed & sucribed
21st August 1816
before
Henry Lane
for R W Lane
