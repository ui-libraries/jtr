John Agnew Esqr
in
Carlisle

69 - 70
11th October 1780
Revd John King &
John McDowell

[back]

Sir

When I was in Carlisle in June I gave you in the names of my
Negros to be registered, I do not recollect that I wrote down their
age, besides I forgot to give in one for my father in Law

Mine are Sue about 25 I suppose & Luce about two years
old - John McDowell a Mulatto wench Poll about 35 years old

These I request you would register according to Law and
oblige your humble Servant
John King
Octr 9th 1780
