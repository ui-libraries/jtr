Francis West Esqr.  
Return of Slaves

Francis West of Tyrone Township in Cumberland  
County & State of Pennsylvania Esquire hath  
this Day delivered in Writing to Clerk of the Peace  
for the said County the Names of all his  
Mullatoe & Negro Slaves Viz-  
Sligoe a Negroe aged about 50 Years  
Jacob a mullato for Life ab<sup><u>t</u></sup>. 34 D<sup>o</sup>  
Poll a Negro Woman ab<sup><u>t</u></sup>. 23 D<sup>o</sup>  
Chamont a Mullatoe ab<sup><u>t</u></sup>. 9 D<sup>o</sup> for Life  
Milla a Mullatoe Girle ab<sup>t</sup> 3 D<sup>o</sup> for Life  
Lewis a Mestoe male child born November 10<sup>th</sup>. 1779 }  
John ~~& Deb~~ A Negro ab<sup>t</sup> 29 Years <ins>for Life</ins>  
Debby about 50 Years a Negro Woman  
August 22^d^  
Fran<sup>s</sup> West
