21^st^ Sep<sup><u>r</sup></u> 1780
Stephen Duncan
return 1 Slave

\[back]

In pursuance of the "Act for the gradual 
Abolition of Slavery" Stephen Duncan of the 
town of Carlisle in the County of Cumberland 
Merchant, doth hereby enter with the Clerk 
of the Sessions of the said County

Frank a Negro Man aged 
Twenty two years or there about whom he owns as 
a Slave during his life

To John Agnew Esq<sup><u>r</sup></u>
Clerk of the Peace in
& for the County of Cumberland

1780
